## 1. Brand & Design Foundations

### Color System
- **Primary Color**: `#3a57a1` (Royal Slate Blue)
- **Secondary Palette**: Shades of primary color & complementary accents
  - **Deep Navy / Background Alt**: `#1E2D53` / `#0D1527`
  - **Soft Primary Tint / Surface Light**: `#EBF1FA`
  - **Interactive Accent / Brand Pop**: `#2563EB` / `#4F46E5`
  - **Warm Contrast Accent** (from Framer inspiration): `#FF6B3D` / `#E8592A` (ideal for active badges, notification pills, and highlights)
  - **Neutrals**: `#FFFFFF` (pure white), `#F8FAFC` (slate 50), `#F1F5F9` (slate 100), `#94A3B8` (muted slate), `#0F172A` (deep slate)

### Typography & Aesthetic Inspiration
- **Primary Design Inspiration**: [Slight Train Framer App](https://slight-train-797942.framer.app/)
  - **Aesthetic Feel**: Ultra-modern, editorial-tech minimalism with organic pill badges, elegant typography, generous whitespace, smooth micro-interactions, dynamic timeline step-by-step guides, and bento-grid card structures.
  - **Fonts**: Inter, Sora / Spline Sans Mono / Fragment Mono (used for time badges, step counters, and technical labels).
- **Secondary Content & Data Inspiration**: [Vizitor App](https://www.vizitorapp.com/)
  - **Workplace Modules & Enterprise Features**: Modules breakdown, security workflows, persona-based capabilities (Admin, HR, Security, Employees), and transparent pricing tiers.

### Fold 6: How to Use (Step-by-Step Workflow)
*Inspiration: [Framer](https://slight-train-797942.framer.app/)*
- **Tag Badge**: `● 11:30 · Seamless 4-Step Process`
- **Heading (H2)**: From ID Scan to Entry Pass
- **Interactive Device + Timeline Component**:
  - **Step 1: Scan the ID** — Scan the visitor's CNIC or driving license.
  - **Step 2: Fetch Visitor Details** — Visitor information is retrieved automatically.
  - **Step 3: Verify the Details** — The guard checks and confirms the information.
  - **Step 4: Generate a QR Pass** — A QR code is generated for the visitor's entry.

*Design: Framer 3-Card Card Aesthetic | Data: Vizitorapp*

- **Tiers**:

     - Price: ₹1,600 / month / location ($19/mo equivalent)

     - Check-ins: 300 check-ins / month
     - Scope: Up to 50 employees, single location
     - Features: QR check-ins, push/email notifications, CSV export, digital badge
  2. **Premium (Recommended / Most Popular)**
     - Price: ₹3,200 / month / location ($39/mo equivalent)
     - Annual: Billed annually with 3 months free

     - Features: Watchlist screening, custom badges, OTP auth, instant host approvals, analytics

     - Price: Custom Quote / Connect with Sales
